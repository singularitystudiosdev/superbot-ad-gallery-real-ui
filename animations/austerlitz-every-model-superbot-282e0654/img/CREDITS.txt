AUSTERLITZ (austerlitz-every-model-superbot-282e0654, added 2026-09-27)
This spot was forked from lastinvention-every-model-superbot-dda6c235; none of that spot's footage or stills
are left here. img/az/ is from the film AUSTERLITZ (2 December 1805 · The Battle of the
Three Emperors) posted by Winter (@WinterArc2125), https://x.com/WinterArc2125/status/2103116235009347650, made in
code in https://github.com/WinterArc21/Battle-of-Austerlitz-Film (main @ 86dae32d). Source file: the repo master
video/austerlitz.mp4 (1920x1080, 24 fps, 7232 frames, 301.333 s). The post's 1920x1080 variant
(https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2103113452726140928/vid/avc1/1920x1080/7kwgLDfFWkBBQ35Z.mp4) has the same
7232 frames: frame n of the post matches frame n of the master at 45 to 48 dB PSNR (27 to 44 dB one frame off), so
it is a re-encode of the master and every still here is cut from the master. Every file in az/ is a crop and
lanczos resize of one real frame, or data measured on the frames and read from the repo source. Nothing was drawn,
painted or generated. Crops are w x h @ (x,y) in 1920x1080 source pixels; times are source seconds (frame = t x
24). Hour and general plates are 16:9 crops inside the picture band, taken outside the caption windows so no
caption text is in them; card-* files are the whole frame, letterbox included, as reference stills of the film's
own captions and cards.
