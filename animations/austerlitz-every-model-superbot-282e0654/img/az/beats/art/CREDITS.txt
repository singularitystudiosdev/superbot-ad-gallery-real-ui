img/az/beats/art: the art beat's nine plates (scenes/tabs-assets/beats/art.js), one per env.js preset the film uses.

Source for every file: /tmp/austerlitz.282e0654/A/austerlitz-master.mp4, the Battle of Austerlitz film master
(1920x1080, 24 fps, 7232 frames, video stream start 0), rendered by WinterArc21/Battle-of-Austerlitz-Film @86dae32d.
Nothing drawn: each file is one film frame, frame-exact (ffmpeg -ss (n-0.5)/24 -frames:v 1; frame n has pts n/24),
cropped to the 2.35:1 picture band between the film's letterbox bars, crop rect x,y,w,h = 0,131,1920,818 (rows 131..948),
then scaled lanczos to 480x204, JPEG -q:v 3. The black bars around each plate in the beat are CSS (#000, the film's bars).
Every t sits outside the film's caption windows and crossfades (Facts settled by A), except art-predawn.jpg, which is the
title card and carries the film's own title text (film.js:216).

Preset per frame is read from web/film.js at the shot playing at t, with scene starts from web/script.js and
data timings; the plate's label in the beat is the preset key and its az/el as web/engine/env.js defines it.
