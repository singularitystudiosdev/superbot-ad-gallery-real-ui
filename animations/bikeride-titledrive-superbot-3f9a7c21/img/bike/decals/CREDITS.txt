each returned a 1024x1024 JPEG on flat white. Six calls, one image each, no retries; ~1120 image tokens per image.

Background cut to real alpha, no painting or retouching:

  - unioned with a white key (distance from the white backdrop), speckle opened away,
  - near-white paint kept only where Vision claims it or an outline encloses it, as a region that is actually
    tinted (this keeps the loose sakura petals Vision dropped, and drops white pockets between leaflets),
  - the outer 3px matted by distance from white against the painted outline and the white un-matted out (no halo),
  - trimmed, ~5% margin, resampled (Lanczos) to 512x512 RGBA PNG.
