peaks.js
--------
Regenerate with: node gen/sfx/make-peaks.mjs   (needs ffmpeg on PATH)
Each clip is decoded to mono 16 kHz PCM, leading/trailing silence under -54 dBFS is trimmed, and the
rest is cut into 56 buckets; each bucket's peak level on a -54..0 dBFS scale is one waveform bar.
The beat reads only peaks.js, so every bar is measured audio, not a drawn shape.
