YOUTUBE STUDIO UI AND PALETTE (sources of record)
  Flow and labels: YouTube Help "Add subtitles & captions" https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796 (fetched
  2026-10-05): "From the left menu, select Subtitles"; "Click ADD LANGUAGE and select your language"; "Under
  'Subtitles', click ADD"; "Select Upload file"; "Choose between With timing or Without timing". The status bar's route
  (English > Add > Upload file > With timing) and the table's Subtitles column (Add, then Published) follow it.
  Palette: YouTube's own --yt-sys-color-baseline--* light values, read from the stylesheets www.youtube.com serves
  (headless Chromium, 2026-10-03): base-background #fff, text-primary #0f0f0f, text-secondary #606060, outline
  rgba(0,0,0,.1), outline-opaque #e5e5e5, additive-background rgba(0,0,0,.05), call-to-action #065fd4. Set in
  chat.css --yt-*.
