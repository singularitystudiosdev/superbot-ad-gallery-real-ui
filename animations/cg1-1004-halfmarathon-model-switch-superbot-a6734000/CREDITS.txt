STRAVA SURFACES (drawn in HTML/CSS/SVG in strava.a6734000.js and map.a6734000.js)
  Route Builder layout follows Strava's help-centre screenshot in "Routes on Web",
  https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401971-routes-on-web (panel: STRAVA Routes, My Routes / New,
  Build your route, Start, numbered waypoints, End, Add waypoint, Routing preferences; map toolbar with Save).
  The save step ("Save to My Routes") and the My Routes card (date, star, menu, underlined name, distance, time,
  elevation) follow https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401678-how-do-i-find-my-saved-routes-on-the-strava-website.
  Why routes and not a calendar: Strava's own training plans moved to Runna
  (https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401942) and Strava has no import for third-party planned workouts,
  so superbot saves each long run as a route (Route Builder needs a Strava subscription).
  The neighbourhood map, its streets, lake, parks and labels are invented and hand-built in SVG.
