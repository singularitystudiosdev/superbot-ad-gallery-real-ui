This spot ships no photographic or third party image assets. Everything on screen - the hub chrome, the app
tiles, the four phase trading panel, its bars, its equity line, its tickets and the confetti burst - is drawn in
CSS/SVG/canvas from code in this folder.

Every number is invented demo data. The backtest scores (return, win rate, drawdown) for Fade the crowd, News
spike chase, Late favourite and Edge over 4% vs fair price are fictional, as are the 1,912 markets and the 90
day book window.

The paper run and the live run are two separate account simulations, and the live one did worse:
- paper account, $5,000 of play money, 14 days, closed at $5,520 (+$520) on the curve the paper phase draws;
- live account, $2,000 stake, the same 14 days and the same rules, realized +$142 on 31 fills (17 winners, 14
  losers, 55% win rate), with a max drawdown read off its own live curve. Live ran below paper after fees and
  slippage.
The open position (Fed cut, yes) and every ticket, price and amount shown are invented too. Nothing here is a
quote, an offer, a promise of return or a claim about superbot.

Polymarket is named as plain text only, with a plain letter tile; no venue logo, exchange mark or chain mark
appears anywhere, and the rail's own third party app tiles are not rendered in this spot. The advice on the
first card belongs to a fictional Claude voice, not to the companies behind these names.
