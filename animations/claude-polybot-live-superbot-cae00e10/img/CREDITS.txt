This spot ships no photographic or third party image assets. Everything on screen - the hub chrome, the app
tiles, the four phase trading panel, its bars, its equity line, its tickets and the confetti burst - is drawn in
CSS/SVG/canvas from code in this folder.

Every number is invented demo data: the four backtest scores (return, win rate, drawdown) for Fade the crowd,
News spike chase, Late favourite and Edge over 4% vs fair price; the paper account (opened at $5,000 of play
money, closed at $6,046) and its daily equity path; the live order ticket; and the scoreboard (realized P&L
+$1,046, win rate 57% on 28 wins and 21 losses, max drawdown read off the paper curve, one open market
position). The 1,912 markets and the 90 day book window are fictional. Nothing here is a quote, an offer, a
promise of return or a claim about superbot.

Polymarket is named as plain text only, with a plain letter tile; no venue logo, exchange mark or chain mark
appears anywhere, and the rail's own third party app tiles are not rendered in this spot. The advice on the
first card belongs to a fictional Claude voice, not to the companies behind these names.
