img/blender/ provenance

All images in this folder were rendered in Blender 5.2.2 LTS (macOS, headless: blender -b -P mic.py) on 2026-10-04
from a procedural bpy script written for this ad. No third-party models, textures, HDRIs or photos were used.
The model is a generic, unbranded USB condenser mic: beveled matte-black cylindrical body, silver mesh grille
(capsule grid with a Wireframe modifier, 0.0011 m), thin orange emissive accent ring, short black three-leg
desk tripod, front gain knob, rear USB-C port.

Scene (evaluated after modifiers via the depsgraph): 15 objects (9 meshes, 4 area lights, 1 camera, 1 empty),
10,349 vertices, 12,428 faces, 25,488 triangles.
Objects: Mic_Body (children: Accent_Ring, Capsule_Core, Gain_Knob, Grille, Grille_Band, USB_C_Port), Tripod,
Studio_Floor, Orbit_Rig (children: Camera, Fill_Light, Key_Light, Rim_Light, Rim_Light_L).

Renderer: EEVEE (BLENDER_EEVEE), 64 render samples, ray tracing on, JPEG quality 85.
- wire.jpg   960x540  viewport-style wireframe (Wireframe modifier on every mesh, emission lines, Standard view transform)
- clay.jpg   960x540  neutral grey clay material override, soft area lights, AgX view transform
- turn/00.jpg..23.jpg 960x540  final materials, dark studio floor, orange rim lights (light-linked to the mic only),
             camera and lights orbit 15 degrees per frame (24 frames = 360 degrees), Khronos PBR Neutral view transform
- hero.jpg  1920x1080 low-angle close-up, 70 mm lens, lens shift so the mic sits in the right two thirds, f/4 depth of field
Total render time for all 27 images: about 47 seconds on this Mac.
