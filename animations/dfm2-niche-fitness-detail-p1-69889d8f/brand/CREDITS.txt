  The authorize sheet follows Strava's real authorization page as shown in Strava's developer docs
  (https://developers.strava.com/docs/getting-started/, images/getting-started-6.png: "Authorize <app> to connect to
  Strava", "<app> will be able to:", "View data about your public profile (required)", "View data about your
  activities", Authorize, Cancel). The third line ("Upload your activities from superbot to Strava") names the
  activity:write scope (https://developers.strava.com/docs/authentication/); its exact on-page wording was not
  verifiable without logging in.
