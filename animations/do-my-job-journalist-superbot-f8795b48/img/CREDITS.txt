AP Stylebook entries (the 12 rules on the LEARN beat and behind the AP style fixes: entry name, the words
quoted, source URL). Quoted verbatim from AP's own public pages: the AP Stylebook's official Bluesky account
(@apstylebook.com), AP Stylebook "Ask the Editor" style guidance, and the AP Statement of News Values and
Principles. The AP Stylebook is a publication of The Associated Press; short quotations only, no affiliation implied.
   1. percent  (percent)
      "Use the % sign with a numeral in most cases: 61% of voters, a 5.5% tariff."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3mwlbg6wqxx2p
   2. months  (months)
      "When a month is used with a specific date, abbreviate only Jan., Feb., Aug., Sept., Oct., Nov. and Dec."
      https://apstylebook.com/ask_the_editors/style_guidance
   3. times  (times)
      "Use figures except for noon and midnight. Use a colon to separate hours from minutes: 11 a.m., 1 p.m., 3:30 p.m., 9-11 a.m., 9 a.m. to 5 p.m."
      https://apstylebook.com/ask_the_editors/style_guidance
   4. times  (times-no-00)
      "Our style is Oct. 10 at 4 p.m. (abbreviating the month when used with a specific date, and not using the :00)"
      https://apstylebook.com/ask_the_editors/style_guidance
   5. millions, billions  (millions)
      "Use figures with million, billion or trillion in all except casual uses, such as I'd like to win a billion dollars."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3mameiir6pk2z
   6. millions, billions  (headline-money)
      "In headlines, abbreviate only millions, billions: $5M lawsuit, $1.6B prize."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3mameiksn7v2e
   7. millions, billions  (decimals)
      "Do not go beyond two decimal places."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3mameijlyvf2e
   8. U.S.  (us-headlines)
      "Use periods in the abbreviation U.S. in body copy text. In headlines, it's US with no periods."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3msvz7jk4dg2x
   9. headlines  (headline-numerals)
      "Use numerals; do not spell out numbers except in casual uses or formal names: hundreds instead of 100s; Big Ten; one of the first."
      https://apstylebook.com/ask_the_editors/style_guidance
  10. hyphen  (hyphen)
      "Use hyphens as joiners, such as for compound modifiers: small-business owner."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3lny4rddlfc2g
  11. datelines  (datelines)
      "Datelines on stories should contain a place name, in capital letters, followed in most cases by the name of the state, country or territory where the city is located."
      https://bsky.app/profile/apstylebook.com/post/3mrq6uxf64d2u
  12. bylines and datelines  (dateline-source)
      "A dateline tells the reader where we obtained the basic information for a story."
      https://www.ap.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/THE-ASSOCIATED-PRESS-STATEMENT-OF-NEWS-VALUES-AND-PRINCIPLES-1.pdf
