Nothing else in this folder is third-party. The Clio Manage desk itself is not an image: it is rebuilt in
HTML/CSS/SVG in ../scenes/tabs-assets/clio.{js,css}. Its layout, colours, type and radii come from reference
material kept outside the repo in /tmp/clioref-9c459cb1 (help.clio.com article screenshots of the 2023-2025 app,
and the design-token custom properties in app.clio.com's stylesheet); no screenshot is embedded in the ad. The
nav and toolbar icons are hand-drawn SVG in the solid style Clio uses, not Font Awesome's or Clio's files.

The docket content on the desk is real public record: case captions, case numbers, ECF entries, docket text and
judges' standing-order provisions, each with its source URL in ../scenes/tabs-assets/clio-data.js (CourtListener
RECAP, cand.uscourts.gov, ded.uscourts.gov). The superbot action labels, draft lines and task titles are the ad's
own copy written from those entries. No person's name is invented: the signed-in user is a generic avatar.
