WHAT IS ON SCREEN AND WHY IT IS TRUE
  No person is named anywhere. Each prospect is shown as the title the account's own release gives its quoted or
  appointed executive, plus the company; the avatar is the company's initials. The signed-in seat is "you" (initials
  SD); no personal name, no phone digits (bullets only), mailbox on example.com (not shown). The worked email, voicemail
  and LinkedIn note are the ad's own copy, restating the account's sourced trigger and its date; every number in them
  is in that account's sourced headline or date. Actions are one-sided: no reply, no meeting. Every count on screen
  is the literal number of items on screen (12 tasks, 3 sequences, 12 findings).
  Trigger cards show a plain fact line composed from the sourced headline (release headlines that name a person are
  never shown); a verbatim quote is shown only when it needs no redaction.
  "Local time" is the prospect's clock at the desk's "now" (Tue Sep 29 2026, 10:05 AM US Eastern), computed from the
  sourced HQ city; blank where no HQ is sourced. The dialer timer is time-lapsed UI state. Sequence names ("Funding
  announced", "New revenue leader", "New office opened") are the ad's own labels; their steps use only Outreach's
  documented step-type names.
