Nothing else in this folder is third-party. The Phrase TMS desk itself is not an image: it is rebuilt in
HTML/CSS/SVG in ../scenes/tabs-assets/phrase.{js,css}. Its layout, colours, type and radii come from reference
material kept outside the repo in /tmp/phraseref-e60f1bcc (NOTES.md there): frames of Phrase's official YouTube
webinars (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQjAMHZyRJk and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5oF2meVBKVs),
support.phrase.com Help Center captures (for example https://support.phrase.com/hc/en-us/articles/5709683926812-CAT-Pane-TMS
and https://support.phrase.com/hc/en-us/articles/5743620785948-CAT-web-editor-releases-2026), a render of the live
sign-in page, and the design-token custom properties of Phrase's design system (sds) in
https://eu.phrase.com/idm-ui/_nuxt/entry-f201c38.css. No screenshot is embedded in the ad. The toolbar, rail and
status icons are hand-drawn SVG in the outline style the editor uses, not Phrase's files.

The translation content on the desk is real: U.S. federal publications (public domain under 17 U.S.C. 105) and
their official Spanish versions published by the same agencies. Every English source segment and every Spanish
target is verbatim from the agency's files; term base entries are verbatim from official glossaries (IRS
Publication 850, CuidadoDeSalud.gov, CFPB); style rules are quoted verbatim from the RAE and ASALE Diccionario
panhispánico de dudas. Each record carries its source URL in ../scenes/tabs-assets/phrase-data.js. The HUD's short
rule labels and the two QA corrections ("EE.UU." to "EE. UU.", "sólo" to "solo") are the ad's own copy, each a
plain restatement of the DPD rule it sits on. No person's name is invented: the signed-in user is an initial.

How the strings were checked (2026-09-29): /tmp/translator-ref-e60f1bcc/build.py re-extracts every PDF above with
poppler pdftotext and refuses to write content.json unless each English and Spanish segment occurs verbatim in its
file (line wraps joined by one space), each glossary headword is followed by its equivalent, and each RAE quote occurs
in the saved DPD article. phrase-data.js is generated from content.json by /tmp/phraseref-e60f1bcc/gen-data.mjs.
The on-screen audit (every visible string at every ratio, every 0.1 s) is in /tmp/translator-ref-e60f1bcc/AUDIT.txt.
Page numbers in the CAT pane read "PDF p. N" because they are 1-based PDF page indexes from pdftotext, which differ
from the printed folio in some files (CFPB glossary PDF p. 62 is printed page 61; CMS 10116-S PDF p. 98 is printed
page 96; IRS Publication 850 indexes equal its printed pages).
