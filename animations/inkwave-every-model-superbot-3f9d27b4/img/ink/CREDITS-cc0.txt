These twelve PNGs are real, freely-licensed game assets, downloaded and cropped out of the source packs'
sprite sheets. Nothing here was drawn or generated. Tinting is a recolour of an existing alpha mask, not a
redraw. See CREDITS-video.txt for the X video footage in this folder.

  all six        "Splat Pack" by Kenney Vleugels for Kenney - CC0 1.0
