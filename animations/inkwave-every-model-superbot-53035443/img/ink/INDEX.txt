Inkwave gameplay capture, source /tmp/xad-media-53035443/inkwave.mp4 (1280x720, 60 fps, 147.8 s, 8863 frames).
Every jpg below is a frame or a crop of THAT video. Nothing here is drawn, traced or synthesised.
"second" is the source second, cut with an exact frame seek (frame = second * 60), so the number is the frame the
pixels actually come from. Every crop was measured on that frame and then opened again and checked by eye against its
name before it was kept. Crop rectangles are quoted as x,y,w,h in source pixels.

WHAT THE CAPTURE ACTUALLY CONTAINS (this replaces the earlier timing note, which had the two full frames the wrong way
round). The file is one sped up session with two matches plus pauses:
  0 s a PAUSED menu, 1 to 49 s the first match (the player quits at 49 s), 50 to 86 s a second match opening and a
  second PAUSED menu at 86 s, 87 to 136 s that match to full time, 134 s JUDGING, 138 s the VICTORY splash, 142 to
  147 s the VICTORY results screen with the scoreboard. So gameplay with the in match HUD is EARLY in the file and the
  VICTORY screen is at the END of it: hud.jpg is the gameplay frame and victory.jpg is the results frame.
A Windows desktop is in shot for the whole capture. The results screen at 142 s is clean; by 147 s the desktop's own
app windows (File Explorer, OBS) and the taskbar sit over it, so results-final.jpg has that overlay across the middle.
