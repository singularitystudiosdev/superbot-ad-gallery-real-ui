The four stills in this folder are the build beat's preview panel: the game a sped up agent run is
producing, one frame per build phase, from the greybox pass to the final one. Every one is cut from the
same source video as the rest of img/ink, the "Splatoon Game made by Opus 5.5" post by @JaydenDavisNC,
https://x.com/JaydenDavisNC/status/2103357848961036304 (source file 3840x2160, 60 fps, 147.8 s).
Video by @JaydenDavisNC. No image here is drawn, traced or generated: each is a frame of that capture,
cropped and scaled.

lap-01.jpg  source 104 s, 2880x1620 crop at (480, 270), scaled to 720x405. The kraken pier boardwalk:
            lime squid on magenta turf, the KRAKEN sign on the far stall, bridge and bay behind.
            The preview's first still: the map the build opens on, shown as a greybox pass.
lap-02.jpg  source 60 s, 2880x1620 crop at (760, 430), scaled to 720x405. The inkling laying a lime
            trail across the magenta pier. The preview's ink pass.
lap-03.jpg  source 14 s, 2880x1620 crop at (240, 180), scaled to 720x405. Squid form on the wall beside
            the pier posters. The preview's character pass.
lap-04.jpg  source 134 s, 2880x1620 crop at (480, 520), scaled to 720x405. The JUDGING turf bar:
            lime 41.5% against magenta 18.7%. The preview's final frame.

The dark plate behind each still, the grey-to-colour grade and the caption are CSS on top of these files
(beats/opus-lapse.css .ocx-pv), written from t by beats/opus-lapse.js. They are not baked into the JPEGs.
The panel's URL line reads localhost:5173 while the build runs and inkwave-six.vercel.app once the deploy
chip lands; that deploy URL is the one the post advertises.
