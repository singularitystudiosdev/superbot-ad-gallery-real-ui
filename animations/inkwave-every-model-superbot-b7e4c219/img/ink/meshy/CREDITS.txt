img/ink/meshy CREDITS (inkwave-every-model-superbot)

Every webp in this folder is a render of a real downloadable 3D model, produced offline with a
headless three.js harness (three.js 0.180 through Playwright/Chrome, one static server on a
private 18700-range port). The harness and the nine source GLBs are kept out of the shipped tree
at .tmp/msy/ (see .tmp/msy/README.md to re-bake). The harness bakes
three passes per angle: clay (single neutral material, the untextured viewport look), wireframe
(cyan triangle shell on the dark studio floor) and textured (the model's own materials, with the
named ink materials recoloured lime / magenta). 36 frames per revolution at 896x558, downscaled
to 448x279 webp (quality 80). No image in this folder is a screenshot, a photograph or a
hand-drawn illustration.

The models, all freely downloadable from Poly Pizza, whose model pages carry the licence and the
author for each:

CC0 carries no attribution duty, but every author is named here so the source is on the record,
and the two CC-BY 3.0 models are credited here as their licences require. Triangle counts shown

Renders were framed to the beat's own slot: 448x279, one subject centred, meshy's viewport recipe
(soft key from the upper front right, cool rim behind, low fill, dark room, and a plain studio floor
that fog fades into the background, so no grid line or grazing-angle moire crosses the frame).
