THE MODEL THE BEAT DEPICTS: HY-Motion 1.0, Tencent Hunyuan's text-to-3D motion model
  It is a series of text-to-3D human motion generation models on a Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with Flow Matching.
  It generates skeleton-based 3D character animation from a text prompt, which is what the beat shows: a rigging step,
  then four clips generated from four prompts. Released 2025-12-30 (1.0B standard, plus a 0.46B Lite).
  Checked and picked 2026-09-26, over the alternatives in the brief (Meshy Animate, Uthana, DeepMotion), because it is
  the current text-to-3D-motion model with a public model page and an open repo, and because step 3 of this spot
  already sourced Meshy for the meshes.
  Official GitHub repository: https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0 (fetched 2026-09-26)
  Official model page:       https://huggingface.co/tencent/HY-Motion-1.0 (fetched 2026-09-26)
  Official project site:     https://hunyuanmotion.net/ (linked as "Official Site" from the model page; the page
                             itself did not render for this fetch on 2026-09-26, so it is recorded but not relied on)
  Paper: HY-Motion 1.0: Scaling Flow Matching Models for Text-To-Motion Generation, arXiv 2512.23464
  Named on screen in beats/motion.js as "HY-Motion 1.0" / "by Tencent Hunyuan". HY-Motion and Tencent Hunyuan are
  trademarks of Tencent; the mark below is used nominatively, to name the model the ad is routing to, and unaltered
  apart from the crop, the circular alpha cut and the 256px resize noted per file.

THE CLIPS: every frame is cut from the "Splatoon Game made by Opus 5.5" post by @JaydenDavisNC,
  https://x.com/JaydenDavisNC/status/2103357848961036304 (source file 3840x2160, 60 fps, 147.8 s; cut from
  /tmp/inkwave/src1080.mp4, the 1920x1080 60 fps intermediate). Video by @JaydenDavisNC. Each clip is 2.4 s,
  H.264 high, crf 30, faststart, no audio, 480x270 (a 960x540 crop of the 1920x1080 frame, halved, so the clip is
  framed on the character rather than the whole HUD), 144 frames at 60 fps, about 120 to 265 KB.
  swim.mp4 / swim.jpg     source 99.0 s to 101.4 s, crop 960x540 at (530, 540): the lime squid form gliding through
                          the ink on the pier, tentacles out, with the ink trail behind it.
  jump.mp4 / jump.jpg     source 105.8 s to 108.2 s, crop 960x540 at (600, 430): the super jump, the lime ink jet

  strafe.mp4 / strafe.jpg source 58.2 s to 60.6 s, crop 960x540 at (330, 435): the inkling strafing with the
                          Splattershot, firing a lime beam across the magenta pier.
  splat.mp4 / splat.jpg   source 34.6 s to 37.2 s, crop 960x540 at (180, 300): the ink burst and the "Splatted by
                          Juno" respawn card that follows it, which is what "get splatted" looks like in this game.
  rig.jpg                 source 99.4 s, 560x560 crop at (730, 590) of the 1920x1080 frame, 480x480 q4. The still the
                          beat auto-rigs: the squid kid in squid form on the ink, which is the same moment as
                          swim.mp4's first second. The skeleton the beat draws over it (7 joints, 6 bones) was laid
                          on this frame by hand, measured off it, and is drawn live in motion.js, never baked in.
  The four .jpg beside the four .mp4 are each that clip's own first frame (the poster the video shows before it plays).

  What is NOT sourced: nothing. Every pixel in this folder is either the model's own published mark or a frame of the
  source video. The motion the four clips show is the real gameplay in that video, cut and reframed; the beat presents
  them as the four clips the model generated from the four prompts, which is the spot's fiction, and there is no
  HY-Motion render anywhere in this folder.
