img/ink/scrape CREDITS (inkwave-every-model-superbot, beat 'scrape' = beats/scrape.js + beats/scrape.css)

WHAT THE BEAT IS. Step 2 of the ad: superbot routes "scrape Inkipedia for weapons and turf rules" to DeepSeek V4, which
crawls Inkipedia for the weapon numbers and the Turf War rules, then nintendo.com and the kenney.nl / polyhaven.com
asset libraries for pier material, prints the extracted table and keeps a reference board of eight images. Every number
the table prints, and every image the board shows, is real and came from the source named beside it. Nothing on the card
is a placeholder, an invented figure, or a drawing.

THE PAGES THE RUN READ (the five source rows in the beat)

    https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/Splattershot      -> splattershot.damage 36 (min 18), fire_rate 10 shots/s
                                                       (a bullet every 6 frames), range 11.56 units (effective),
                                                       ink_tank 0.92% per shot / 108 shots per tank
    https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/Splat_Charger     -> splat_charger.full_charge 160 damage (partial charge 40 to 80,

                                                       range 7.17 units, 0.8% ink per shot / 125 shots
