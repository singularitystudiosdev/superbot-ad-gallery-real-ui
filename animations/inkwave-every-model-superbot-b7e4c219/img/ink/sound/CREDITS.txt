img/ink/sound - assets for the sound beat (beats/sound.js: ElevenLabs sound effects and Suno's Turf War track,
in parallel lanes).

suno.svg
  The Suno mark, downloaded from Simple Icons (https://cdn.simpleicons.org/suno, simpleicons.org, released CC0).
  Used as the tile in the beat's Suno lane header and in its "Composing Turf War (Kraken Pier)" chip, because no
  Suno logo was on disk when this beat was built (chat.js APPS.suno.logo is still null, so the routing chip and the
  reply header still draw the app initial). Model label: v6, Suno's current model family ("Introducing the v6 family
  of music models", suno.com/release-notes, 9 September 2026).

turf-war-spectrogram.webp
  A real spectrogram of the source spot's own audio track, rendered with ffmpeg (showspectrumpic, 40 Hz to 8 kHz,
  log frequency scale, magma) from 117-141 s of /tmp/inkwave/inkwave.mp4, the @JaydenDavisNC "Splatoon Game made by
  Opus 5.5" post. It stands in for the finished Suno track inside the ad: the Suno lane reveals it left to right as
  the track composes. The source audio is very quiet (about -56 dBFS RMS), so it was lifted +45 dB before analysis;
  the structure in the picture (the build, the two gaps, the loud last section) is really there in the recording.

audio-data.js
  Measured numbers off the same audio track: ffmpeg decode to 16 kHz mono, then a 5 ms peak envelope (SFX_BARS) and
  48-bucket band RMS (STEM_BARS). SFX_BARS holds the nine sharpest real transients in the track, each sliced to the
  length its sound-effect row states, so every waveform the left lane records in is that clip's own envelope and not
  a drawing. STEM_BARS holds four frequency bands (bass 20-160 Hz, guitar 160-700, vocal 700-2600, air 2600-8000)
  over 117-141 s, which is where the arrangement builds and then drops. Regenerate:
    ffmpeg -i inkwave.mp4 -ac 1 -ar 16000 -f s16le mono.pcm   # then a 5 ms peak / 48-bucket RMS reduction
    ffmpeg -ss 117 -t 24 -i inkwave.mp4 -af "highpass=f=20,lowpass=f=160,volume=50dB" -ac 1 -ar 8000 -f s16le band-bass.pcm
