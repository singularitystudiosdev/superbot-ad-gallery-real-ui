img/ink/sound: the sound beat's audio analysis (beats/sound.js). Every waveform in that beat is drawn from the peak
arrays in peaks.json, and every peak in peaks.json is a real sample of the X post's own audio.

SOURCE AUDIO
  /tmp/xad-media-f1a86d6f/post720.mp4 = x.com/JaydenDavisNC/status/2103357848961036304, AAC audio, 48 kHz stereo,
  147.797 s (the post's own game capture: Splatoon-like "Inkwave" turf war).

METHOD (reproducible, no generated data)
  1. ffmpeg -v error -i post720.mp4 -ac 1 -ar 8000 -f s16le post8k.raw
  2. per span: 48 bars, each bar = max |sample| in its bin, normalised to the span's own loudest bin (floor 0.02)
  3. stems: the bed span band-split with ffmpeg (drums highpass 3k, bass lowpass 250, synth 250 Hz-1.2 kHz,
     vocal chops 1.2-3 kHz, air highpass 6k), same per-bin peak measure, 40 bars; the mixdown row draws the bed span's
     own peaks, unfiltered
  4. written to peaks.json by the extractor kept at /tmp/xad-sound-f1a86d6f/extract.mjs (a build tool, not part of the ad)
