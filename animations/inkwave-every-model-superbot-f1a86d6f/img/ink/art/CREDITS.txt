img/ink/art/ CREDITS: the art beat (scenes/tabs-assets/beats/art.js).

WHAT THESE ARE. Eight real stills, each one a rectangular crop of a single frame of the post's own gameplay video.
Nothing in this folder is hand-drawn, recoloured, re-rendered, upscaled, generated by an image model, or taken from
any other source: every pixel is a pixel of the video, and the crop boxes below are the whole recipe. The beat shows
each one resolving out of a coarse mosaic of its own pixels (a 24px resample of the same file), so the "generation"
read is the file coming into focus, not a placeholder or a drawn shape.

THE SOURCE. X post by JaydenDavisNC, "Splatoon Game made by Opus 5.5" (game: Inkwave, inkwave-six.vercel.app),
https://x.com/JaydenDavisNC/status/2103357848961036304. Same post, same upload as img/ink/play/ and img/ink/sound/.
The 1280x720 copy the rest of the ad works from is /tmp/xad-media-f1a86d6f/post720.mp4; these crops are cut from the
post's own 1920x1080 H.264 variant, the highest <=1080p rendition listed for the upload by
https://api.fxtwitter.com/JaydenDavisNC/status/2103357848961036304:
  https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2103357145110474752/vid/avc1/1920x1080/ffgYcFZtkGjMQrUv.mp4  (1920x1080,
  10.4 Mbps, 147.72s) - downloaded once to the sourcing scratch, never served by the ad.
The upload also offers 3840x2160 (25.1 Mbps) and lower 720p / 640x360 / 480x270 renditions. A 1080p still is used
rather than a screenshot of the 720p file because the HUD icons, the poster lettering and the KRAKEN fascia are all
small on screen: cropping at 1.5x the pixels keeps the lettering that makes each tile readable.

HOW TO RE-CUT. sh .tmp/art-f1a86d6f/crop.sh <path-to-1080p.mp4> <this-dir> replays every crop below from the source
video with ffmpeg (decode a frame at the timestamp) and ImageMagick (cut the box, write webp quality 88). The
timestamps are seconds into the post.

noiz/ holds the placeholder each tile in beats/art.js resolves out of: the same crop at 24 pixels wide (ImageMagick
-resize 24x, webp quality 90), stretched back over the 134px tile by the browser with image-rendering:pixelated. So
the "generation" the beat shows is one real file coming into focus out of its own downsampled self, not a drawn or
invented placeholder. One noiz file per crop, same base name.

WHY THESE MOMENTS. The post is one 2:27 turf-war match, so the wall of posters (14s), the KRAKEN board (30.5s) and
the DOUBLE SPLAT! banner (31s) are the moments the frame actually holds them; the ink decals are cut from 12s and
16s where ink is spread flat across paving and reads as a shape rather than a texture on a slope. The brief for this
beat named exactly these five subjects (the LOW TIDE RIOT poster and the posters on its wall, the KRAKEN billboard,
the lime and magenta splat decals, the ink tank and Special ready meter, the team squid icons and the DOUBLE SPLAT!
banner), and this folder is those subjects, cropped.
