img/ink/motion/ - every pixel is a real 3D render, no screenshots and no drawn art
================================================================================

WHAT THIS FOLDER HOLDS
  swim-NN.webp     72 frames, 30fps (2.4s)   the "dive into ink and swim" motion
  splat-NN.webp    72 frames, 30fps (2.4s)   the "strafe and fire the splattershot" motion
  jump-NN.webp     72 frames, 30fps (2.4s)   the "super jump launch" motion
  victory-NN.webp  72 frames, 30fps (2.4s)   the "victory pose on the podium" motion
  juno-rig.webp    one still, 224x168        the carried rig thumbnail the card opens on

  NN is 00..71. Frames are 224x168 webp, quantised from a headless three.js render.

HOW THEY WERE MADE
  Renderer: three.js r180 (three/examples/jsm/loaders/GLTFLoader.js) in headless Chromium via Playwright,
  swiftshader backend, 224x168, ACESFilmic tone mapping. The renderer, its scene lighting and the exact
  per-clip camera/pose arguments are kept beside this ad at .tmp/motion-f1a86d6f/harness/rig.html +
  shoot_rig.mjs (gitignored); the GBL files it loads sit in the same folder.
  Nothing in the beat runtime draws anything: the ad plays these baked webp frames as a pure function of t.

CHARACTER (the rig HY-Motion is handed as juno.glb)
  Rogue_Hooded.glb
    KayKit Adventurers 2.0, "Hooded Rogue" chibi character, by Kay Lousberg (KayKit).
    CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). kaylousberg.itch.io
    Pack: github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
    File reached through the CC0 mirror github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-kaykit-3d-characters
    (assets/kaykit/adventurers-2.0/Characters/gltf/Rogue_Hooded.glb). 1 skinned mesh set, 32 nodes.
    Rendered here as a 55% opacity ghost over its own texture atlas, tinted lime/teal (#9fe6c4) so it reads
    as the ad's Juno.

ANIMATION (the motions HY-Motion is asked for)
  KayKit Character Animations 1.1, same author, CC0 1.0, same repo mirror, folder
  assets/kaykit/character-animations-1.1/Animations/gltf/Rig_Medium/. These clips are authored on the
  identical rig (same bone names), so they are played straight onto the character with THREE.AnimationMixer
  and sampled at t = frame/30. The four motions are:
    swim     Crawling              (Rig_Medium_MovementAdvanced.glb)
    splat    Ranged_1H_Shooting    (Rig_Medium_CombatRanged.glb)
    jump     Jump_Full_Long        (Rig_Medium_MovementBasic.glb)
    victory  Cheering              (Rig_Medium_Simulation.glb)
  The harness also loads Rig_Medium_Special.glb; 68 clips from those five sets are attached to the rig, which
  is the "68 clips" the card names.

THE LIME SKELETON
  Not a THREE.SkeletonHelper: the overlay is real geometry built by the harness, a joint sphere on every bone
  and a cylinder shaft between each bone and its parent, each parented to the bone itself so it follows the
  skinning exactly, drawn depthTest-free in lime #b6f000 / #d6ff4d over the ghost body. Rim lights are the
  ad's own ink colours: lime #b6f000 behind right, magenta #e5189a behind left. The clay tint colour, the
  ghost opacity and the rim lights are authored lighting; the mesh, the skin weights and the animation curves
  are all the CC0 assets above.

  No Tencent/Hunyuan asset, API key or generated file is used anywhere in this folder: HY-Motion 1.0 is named
  in the copy as the model the ad routed the request to, and the motion footage is the render above.
