WHAT THE CARD CLAIMS, AND WHAT IS REAL. The four searches, the page counts (splatoonwiki.org 22, polyhaven.com 63,
kenney.nl 37, opengameart.org 58, 180 total) and the pick order are staged for the spot, like every other beat's card.
The pictures are not staged: each one is the real preview image of a real asset, fetched from the library the chip names.

1. POLY HAVEN, polyhaven.com (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication; https://polyhaven.com/license). HDRI skies and
   PBR texture previews, taken off the site's own CDN thumbnail endpoint
   (https://cdn.polyhaven.com/asset_img/thumbs/<slug>.png). The small mirror-ball/light-probe inset visible in the HDRI
   frames is Poly Haven's own preview scene, part of the downloaded thumbnail. Eight files:

     hdri-secluded-beach.webp   <- secluded_beach          HDRI, coastal dunes and open water, midday sun
     hdri-fish-hoek-beach.webp  <- fish_hoek_beach         HDRI, beach at low tide under an overcast sky
     hdri-small-harbor.webp     <- small_harbor_01         HDRI, small harbor at sunrise
     hdri-simons-town.webp      <- simons_town_harbour     HDRI, harbour with a moored boat, morning
     tex-pavers.webp            <- concrete_pavers_02      texture, reddish concrete pavers
     tex-pavement.webp          <- concrete_pavement_03    texture, dark concrete pavement slabs
     tex-concrete-worn.webp     <- concrete_floor_worn_001 texture, worn grey concrete floor
     tex-checkered-paving.webp  <- checkered_pavement_tiles texture, checkered pavement tiles

   These four skies and four grounds are the palette the plaza is blocked out in (seaside light, concrete plaza floor).

2. KENNEY, kenney.nl (CC0 1.0; the packs' own License.txt ships with each download and names Kenney as the author,
   credit not mandatory). The kit preview sheets, cropped from 918x515 to 918x440 to trim Kenney's own sample
   watermark strip (its CC0 badge and wordmark) off the bottom edge. Nothing inside the artwork is altered. Three files:

     kenney-nature.webp        <- Nature Kit sample.png        palms and tropical props on a teal island
     kenney-survival.webp      <- Survival Kit sample.png      jungle, palm trees and supply crates
     kenney-tower-defense.webp <- Tower Defense Kit sample.png crates, rocks and towers

   The palm tree and the wooden crate the map needs are the props these kits supply; the Meshy beat models the ad's
   own versions of them (img/ink/meshy/CREDITS.txt).
