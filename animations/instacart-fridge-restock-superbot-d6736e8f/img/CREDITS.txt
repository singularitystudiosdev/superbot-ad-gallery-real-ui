All images in this folder were made for this ad (2026-10-05) with Google Nano Banana Pro
(google/gemini-3-pro-image-preview, called through OpenRouter). No stock photography, no third-party footage.

fridge.jpg, fridge-thumb.jpg   the open, nearly empty fridge (empty egg carton, near-empty oat milk carton, one yogurt cup,
                               butter wrapper with a last sliver, cheddar end piece, empty crisper). Generated at
                               1792x2400, then one image-edit pass to shrink the butter to a sliver and clear the faint
                               print on its wrapper. Scaled to 1200 / 300 px wide.
products/*.jpg                 seven generic product shots on white (eggs, oat milk carton, spinach clamshell, yogurt
                               tub, lemon bag, butter block, cheddar block). Plain packaging, no brand names or logos.
                               Trimmed and padded to 200x200.
rosa.jpg                       the shopper avatar ("Rosa is shopping"), a generated portrait of a person who does not
                               exist. Cropped to 160x160.
