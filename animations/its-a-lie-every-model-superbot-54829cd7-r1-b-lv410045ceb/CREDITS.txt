  occlusion), script.js, game.js, sheep.js, ui.css, index.html and two test files. The code in both Opus cards is cut

  verbatim. The game's 0.9 s grass break, its grazing sheep and Blender's stone pickaxe come from that spec.
  Only facts were used from minecraft.wiki (CC BY-NC-SA); no text or images from it are shown.
