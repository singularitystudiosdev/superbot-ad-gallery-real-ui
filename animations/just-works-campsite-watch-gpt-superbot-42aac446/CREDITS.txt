Site Watch spot (Upper Pines, Jul 3 to 6), asset credits. Every photo is a real photograph from Wikimedia
Commons, scaled for the page. The user, the account, the other openings, the card, the plate and the
confirmation number in data.js are invented for the spot.

Facts the page states, checked on the live sites:
  Upper Pines Campground, Yosemite National Park, is Recreation.gov campground 232447
    https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232447
  $36 per night: https://www.thedyrt.com/camping/california/california-upper-pines
  $8 online reservation fee per camping reservation: https://www.recreation.gov/rules-reservation-policies
  A carted site is held for 15 minutes:
    https://www.recreation.gov/articles/list/tips-for-making-campsite-reservations-at-popular-locations/281
  Elevation 4,000 ft, picnic table, fire ring and food storage locker at each site, park entry not
  included in the reservation: the campground page above.
