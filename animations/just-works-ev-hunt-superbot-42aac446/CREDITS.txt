Tesla Model Y hunt spot (Tesla store inventory, a Carvana listing, a CarMax listing and a saved CarGurus search,
scanned for a Model Y Long Range under $40k within 200 miles of ZIP 78704 and ranked in one shortlist), asset credits.
The shopper, the search radius, the listings, their VINs, mileages, prices, price drops, FSD status, days on lot and
distances are invented for the spot and live in data.js; the shops named (Tesla stores, Carvana, CarMax, CarGurus,
Tesla account) are real businesses and the Tesla model names and paint names are Tesla's own names for real products.
No VIN, price or listing shown is a real vehicle. The page reads only: it never orders, reserves, cancels, messages
or changes anything at Tesla, Carvana, CarMax or CarGurus, and it never touches the Tesla account. All figures
printed on the page, the hub and the steps are derived from data.js.
