Home audit spot (Home Assistant, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, SmartThings and Philips Hue, one
household read across all five), asset credits. The household, the 5 accounts, the 214 device
entries pulled, the 87 duplicate entries merged into 127 unique devices, the 9 rooms and every
count shown (6 offline, 9 on low battery under 20 percent, 11 behind on firmware, 4 automations
that have not fired in 7 or more days) are invented for the spot and live in data.js. The page
reads only: it never pairs, renames, reconfigures, turns off or deletes anything in Home
Assistant, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings or Philips Hue, and the audit makes no change to any
account. All figures printed on the page are derived from data.js.

Service marks and the app icon, via the Iconify API (api.iconify.design), set on rounded 64 px
tiles. Trademarks of their owners, used only to name the services the audit reads from.
  brand/homeassistant.svg  simple-icons:homeassistant (CC0)
  brand/googlehome.svg     simple-icons:googlehome (CC0)
  brand/alexa.svg          simple-icons:amazonalexa (CC0), Amazon Alexa
  brand/smartthings.svg    simple-icons:smartthings (CC0)
  brand/hue.svg            simple-icons:philipshue (CC0), Philips Hue
  brand/device.svg         mdi:lightbulb-on-outline (Apache 2.0), the row glyph for a device in
                           the audit, on a neutral slate tile
  brand/app.svg            mdi:home-automation (Apache 2.0), tinted mint, the app favicon

Tile fills are each brand's own published colour from the simple-icons set. Home Assistant,
Alexa and SmartThings have bright cyan fills, where a white glyph would not carry, so those
three draw their glyph in the page ink #0B1220; Google Home and Philips Hue keep the white
glyph. The tile fill is unchanged either way. The device and app tiles are the page's own slate
and ink, not brand colours, and carry no trademark.

Inline interface glyphs in site.js (check, lock, refresh, search, filter, clock, calendar,
activity, list, grid, merge, info, alert, bulb, plug, wifi, battery, thermometer, sparkles) are
lucide paths fetched from the same Iconify API. This list starts from the same interface set the
other spots use and may be extended when the page itself is built. No brand product photos are
used: devices are named by model and room, and the row glyph for a device is the drawn mark
above.
