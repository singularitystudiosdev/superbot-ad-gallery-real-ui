Job hunt spot (LinkedIn, Indeed and Gmail plus the Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards of 40
target companies, one senior backend engineer's search read across all of them), asset credits.
The person, the 52 applications, the 83 recruiter emails, the 40 target companies and every
company and role shown (Lumen Pay, Parcelwise, Quayside Data, Kestrel AI and the rest), their
board URLs, dates, statuses and next steps are invented for the spot and live in data.js. Any
resemblance to a real employer is unintended. The page reads only: it never applies, withdraws,
messages, replies, accepts an invite or edits a profile on LinkedIn, Indeed, Gmail or any board.
All figures printed on the page are derived from data.js.

Service marks and the app icon, via the Iconify API (api.iconify.design), set on rounded 64 px
tiles. Trademarks of their owners, used only to name the services the search reads from.
  brand/linkedin.svg    mdi:linkedin (Apache 2.0) on LinkedIn blue #0A66C2
  brand/indeed.svg      simple-icons:indeed (CC0) on Indeed blue #003A9B
  brand/gmail.svg       simple-icons:gmail (CC0) on Gmail red #EA4335
  brand/greenhouse.svg  simple-icons:greenhouse (CC0) on Greenhouse green #24A47F
  brand/board.svg       mdi:briefcase-search (Apache 2.0) on a neutral slate tile, the mark for the
                        Lever and Ashby boards: neither ships a mark in an open icon set, so they
                        are named in text and get this neutral job-board glyph, not a logo
  brand/role.svg        mdi:briefcase-outline (Apache 2.0), the row glyph for an application
  brand/app.svg         mdi:briefcase-check (Apache 2.0), tinted mint, the app favicon

The board, role and app tiles are the page's own slate and ink, not brand colours, and carry no
trademark.

Inline interface glyphs in site.js (list, lock, check, refresh, map pin, building, briefcase,
sparkles, calendar, message, circle x, hourglass, send, link, external link, clock, merge,
shield, circle check) are lucide paths (ISC) fetched from the same Iconify API.
