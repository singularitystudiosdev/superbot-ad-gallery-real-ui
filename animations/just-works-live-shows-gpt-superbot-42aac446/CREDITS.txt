Live shows spot, variant A with the ChatGPT refusal (Spotify followed and most-played artists checked against Ticketmaster, AXS, Dice, Bandsintown, Songkick and local Denver venue sites into one six-month concert overview),
asset credits.
The listener in Denver, Colorado, the Spotify listening, the followed and most-played artists, the
tour dates, the venue dates, the presale and on-sale times, the price ranges and the owned tickets
are invented for the spot and live in data.js; the artists, the venues and the services named
(Spotify, Ticketmaster, AXS, Dice, Bandsintown, Songkick and the Denver venue sites) are real, and no
show listed is a real announced date. All figures printed on the page, the hub and the steps are
derived from data.js.
The page reads only: it never buys, holds or resells a ticket, never joins a presale or a ticket
queue, never follows or unfollows an artist, and never transfers a ticket. Nothing is bought, no
presale is joined and no ticket is transferred.
