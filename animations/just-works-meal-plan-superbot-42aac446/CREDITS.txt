Meal plan spot (saved recipes in Mealie, Paprika and NYT Cooking, live prices, sale flyers and stock
at Kroger, Instacart and Costco, and a Google Calendar week, read into one dinner plan and one
grocery list), asset credits.
The household, the dinners chosen, the recipe counts, the calendar events, the quantities, the
prices, the sale prices, the stock states and every total are invented for the spot and live in
data.js; the apps, services and stores named (Mealie, Paprika Recipe Manager, NYT Cooking, Kroger,
Instacart, Costco and Google Calendar) are real businesses and projects. The recipe titles are
generic dish names, not copies of any published recipe. No account, order, cart or event shown is a
real one.
The page reads only: it never orders anything, never adds anything to a Kroger, Instacart or Costco
cart, never edits or saves a recipe in Mealie, Paprika or NYT Cooking, and never creates, edits or
books anything in Google Calendar. All figures printed on the page, the hub and the steps are
derived from data.js.
