Backlog spot (Steam + Epic + GOG, one backlog), asset credits. The user, the three library sizes, every
playtime and today's wishlist prices in data.js are invented for the spot. HowLongToBeat main-story hours
and Steam review percentages are rounded approximations of the public figures. The page buys nothing:
it is a read-only view of the libraries.
