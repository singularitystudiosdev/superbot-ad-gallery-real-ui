Photo gear spot (a photo gear shortlist whose prices, conditions and stock are read from a B&H Photo
Video, Adorama, MPB, KEH Camera and eBay shelf, against a gear set read from an Adobe Lightroom
catalog), asset credits.
The bodies, lenses, listings, prices, conditions, shutter counts, stock states, graded prices and
shortlist notes shown on the page are invented for the spot and live in data.js; the apps and shops
named (Adobe Lightroom, B&H Photo Video, Adorama, MPB, KEH Camera and eBay) are real businesses, and
the camera and lens names are their owners' own names for real products. No listing, price, grade or
stock figure shown is a real one.
The page reads only: it never buys, bids, makes an offer or adds an item to a cart at B&H, Adorama,
MPB, KEH or eBay, and it never writes to, imports into or grades an Adobe Lightroom catalog. All
figures printed on the page, the hub and the steps are derived from data.js.
