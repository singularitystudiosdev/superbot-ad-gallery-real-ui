3D print farm spot (a Bambu Handy printer list, a Prusa Connect queue and an OctoPrint server read
into one queue board, a Polymaker, Prusament, Bambu Lab store and Amazon filament shelf, and a
MakerWorld and Printables model shortlist), asset credits.
The printers, queues, jobs, spools, models, designers, print times, layer counts, filament weights,
temperatures, progress percentages and prices are invented for the spot and live in data.js; the
services and shops named (Bambu Handy, Prusa Connect, OctoPrint, MakerWorld, Printables, the Bambu
Lab store, Polymaker, Prusament and Amazon) are real businesses, and the printer and filament names
are their owners' own names for real products. No printer, job, spool or model shown is a real one.
The page reads only: it never starts, pauses or cancels a print, never loads or unloads a spool,
never edits a queue or a store, and never buys anything at the Bambu Lab store, Polymaker, Prusament
or Amazon, and it never writes to the Bambu Handy, Prusa Connect or OctoPrint accounts. All figures
printed on the page, the hub and the steps are derived from data.js.
