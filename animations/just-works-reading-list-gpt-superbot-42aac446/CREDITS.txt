Reading list spot, variant A with the ChatGPT refusal (Libby with two library cards, the Goodreads
want-to-read shelf, the Kindle and Audible libraries, and the live availability, hold queue and estimated
wait of 48 want-to-read titles at Seattle Public Library and King County Library System), asset credits.
The reader, the two library cards, the hold positions, the queue figures, the loans and the due dates are
invented for the spot and live in data.js. The titles and authors are real books; the shelves, the holds, the
waits and the due dates are not, and nothing here is what any library system actually reports. Any
resemblance to a real reader's shelf is unintended. The page reads only: it never borrows, places a hold,
joins a waitlist, renews, returns or rates anything on Libby, Goodreads, Kindle or Audible, and it does not
touch either library account. All figures printed on the page are derived from data.js.

Service marks and the app icon, via the Iconify API (api.iconify.design), set on rounded 64 px tiles.
Trademarks of their owners, used only to name the services the shelf is read from.
  brand/goodreads.svg   simple-icons:goodreads (CC0) on Goodreads brown #372213
  brand/audible.svg     simple-icons:audible (CC0) on Audible orange #F8991C
  brand/kindle.svg      simple-icons:amazon (CC0) on Amazon ink #232F3E, the mark for the Kindle library:
                        the Kindle store is Amazon's, and no separate Kindle mark ships in an open icon set
  brand/libby.svg       mdi:book-open-variant (Apache 2.0) on a neutral slate tile, the mark for Libby and
                        for both library cards (Seattle Public Library and King County Library System):
                        Libby ships no mark in an open icon set, so it is named in text and gets this neutral
                        book glyph, not a logo
  brand/book.svg        mdi:book-open-page-variant-outline (Apache 2.0), the row glyph for a title
  brand/app.svg         mdi:bookmark-outline (Apache 2.0), tinted mint, the app favicon

The shelf, book and app tiles are the page's own slate and ink, not brand colours, and carry no trademark.

Inline interface glyphs in site.js (list, lock, check, refresh, map pin, sparkles, calendar check, calendar
days, hourglass, link, external link, clock, merge, shield check, circle check, bookmark, book open, library,
headphones) are lucide paths (ISC) fetched from the same Iconify API.
