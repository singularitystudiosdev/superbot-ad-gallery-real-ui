Study streak spot (Duolingo, AnkiWeb, WaniKani and italki read into one morning study overview),
asset credits.
The learner, the streak counts, the XP, the league standing, the decks, the due counts, the tutor,
the lesson and the homework are invented for the spot and live in data.js; the apps and services
named (Duolingo, Anki, AnkiWeb, WaniKani and italki) are real. No account, streak, deck, card,
lesson, booking or payment shown is a real one.
The page reads only: it never completes a lesson on Duolingo, never reviews, adds or schedules a
card in Anki or AnkiWeb, never opens or completes a review on WaniKani, never books, reschedules or
cancels a lesson with an italki tutor, and never buys a streak freeze or any other in-app item. All
figures printed on the page, the hub and the steps are derived from data.js.
