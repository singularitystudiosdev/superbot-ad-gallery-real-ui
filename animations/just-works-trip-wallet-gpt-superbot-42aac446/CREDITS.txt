Trip wallet spot (Gmail, United Airlines, Delta, Marriott and Airbnb, one traveller's trips
gathered from five accounts), asset credits. The traveller, the 5 accounts, the 25 records pulled
(14 Gmail confirmation emails, 3 United flights, 3 Delta flights, 3 Marriott stays, 2 Airbnb
stays), the 14 duplicate confirmations merged into 11 bookings across 3 trips (Lisbon, Chicago,
Maui), the 7 loyalty balances, and every balance and expiry date shown are invented for the spot
and live in data.js. The page reads only: it never books, changes, cancels or redeems anything in
Gmail, United Airlines, Delta, Marriott or Airbnb, and it makes no change to any account. All
figures printed on the page are derived from data.js.

tiles. Trademarks of their owners, used only to name the accounts the wallet gathers from.
  brand/gmail.svg     simple-icons:gmail (CC0), Gmail

  brand/delta.svg     the Delta widget triangle, taken from "Delta logo.svg" on Wikimedia Commons

  brand/ticket.svg    mdi:ticket-outline (Apache 2.0), the row glyph for other bookings, on a

  brand/app.svg       mdi:airplane-takeoff (Apache 2.0), tinted mint, the app favicon

Tile fills are each brand's own published colour: Gmail #EA4335, United Airlines #002244, Delta
#003366, Marriott #A70023, Airbnb #FF5A5F, American Airlines #0078D2. Every one of those six fills
is dark or saturated enough that a white glyph carries on it, so five of the six carry a white
glyph; Delta's tile is the one exception, drawn as the widget triangle in Delta red #E31837 on the
Delta navy, both of them Delta's own published colours. The plane, hotel and ticket tiles are the
page's own slate #171d2a with a soft slate glyph #c3ccdb, and the app tile is ink #101828 with mint
#7cf2b0. Those four carry no trademark.

Inline interface glyphs in site.js (check, lock, refresh, search, clock, calendar, plane, bed,
ticket, wallet, alert, info, sparkles, map-pin, armchair, arrow-right) are lucide paths fetched
from the same Iconify API. This list starts from the same interface set the other spots use and may
be extended when the page itself is built. No brand product photos are used: bookings are named by
type and amount, and the row glyph for a booking is the drawn mark above.
