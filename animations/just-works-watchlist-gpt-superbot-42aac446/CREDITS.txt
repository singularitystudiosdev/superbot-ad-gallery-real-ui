Watchlist spot, variant A with the ChatGPT refusal (the Letterboxd, IMDb and Trakt watchlists read and
folded into one list of 214 distinct titles, each title checked on Netflix, Max, Prime Video, Hulu and
Disney+: 1,070 checks), asset credits.
The viewer, the three watchlists, the list overlap, the per-service counts, the leaving dates and the rent
prices are invented for the spot and live in data.js. The titles are real films and series and the plan
prices are the US list prices of those plans; which service carries which title, and when it leaves, is not
what any service actually reports. Any resemblance to a real viewer's watchlist is unintended. The page
reads only: it never adds, removes, rates or marks anything on Letterboxd, IMDb or Trakt, and it never
cancels, pauses or changes any subscription. All figures printed on the page, the hub and the steps are
derived from data.js.
