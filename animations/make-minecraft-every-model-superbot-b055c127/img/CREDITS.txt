gen/ref/*.jpg, gen/ref/extra/*.jpg  Colour (albedo) maps of CC0 1.0 photoscanned PBR materials
                                     (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) from ambientCG (by Lennart
                                     Demes, ambientcg.com) and Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), each one the tile the scrape
                                     grid badges with that site. Each is a square crop of the map (centre fraction
                                     noted), resized to 256x256 (Lanczos) and saved as JPEG. For a consistent grid, the
                                     luminance mean and spread were pulled part-way (45%; sand 35%, snow 12%) toward a
                                     shared target; hue and content are untouched. The two cut-out materials (TreeEnd004,
                                     Leaf001) are cropped to the largest square wholly inside their Opacity mask, so no
                                     background or alpha shows.
