Added for mariokart-every-model-superbot-7aec3197 (2026-09-26). Source post: @bridgemindai,
"Opus 5.5 One-Shot a Mario Kart Game", https://x.com/bridgemindai/status/2102451997395866021
(game: Turbo Kart Rally, track: Palm Cove Circuit). Every file in kart/ is a crop, trim or rectification of
real frames of that post's 1080p mp4
(https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2102451221483180032/vid/avc1/2098x1080/vHjQ8jI6WsTR-TPn.mp4,
2098x1080, 52.4s, variable frame rate, no audio). Nothing was drawn, painted or generated. Crops are given as
w x h @ (x,y) in source pixels; timestamps are source seconds.
