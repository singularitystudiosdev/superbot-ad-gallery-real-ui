rew.mp4       the VHS rewind: 57 single frames grabbed at eased source times, played back in order at 30fps
                s_i = S1 - (S1 - 0.4) * inOutQuad(i / 56),  i = 0..56,  S1 = 37.7 + 89/30 = 40.6667 (fwd's last frame)
                inOutQuad(x) = x < .5 ? 2x^2 : 1 - (2 - 2x)^2 / 2   (slow off the pause, fast mid-way, settles on the title)
              so frame 0 = source 40.6667s and frame 56 = source 0.4000s (the TURBO KART RALLY title screen); it runs
              backward through the race, the GO! start, CHOOSE YOUR RACER, to the title. Each frame:
                ffmpeg -ss <s_i> -i clip1080.mp4 -frames:v 1 -vf "scale=1600:-2:flags=lanczos" f<i>.png
              encoded all-intra so every seek is exact: 1600x824, 30fps, h264 crf 20 yuv420p, -g 1, +faststart,
              no audio, 57 frames, 1.900s
                ffmpeg -framerate 30 -i f%02d.png -vf format=yuv420p -an -c:v libx264 -crf 20 -g 1 -keyint_min 1 \
                  -movflags +faststart -r 30 rew.mp4
              The frame times (i s_i): 0 40.6667, 1 40.6410, 2 40.5639, ... 28 20.5333, ... 55 0.4257, 56 0.4000
