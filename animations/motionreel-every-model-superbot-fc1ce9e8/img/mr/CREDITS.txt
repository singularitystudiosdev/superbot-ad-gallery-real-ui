img/mr CREDITS (motionreel-every-model-superbot)

Every file in img/mr/ is cut from one source: the video in this post. Nothing was redrawn, recoloured or
composited. The only processing is frame selection, lanczos scale, one colour matrix conversion for the video
(BT.601 to BT.709, see COLOUR; it restores the source colours, it does not change them) and re-encode. reel.mp4
keeps every frame of the source at its own timestamp (the source is already exactly n/60). The audio track was
dropped.

COLOUR
The source carries no colour tags. Its YUV values are BT.601 limited range: decoded as BT.601 the flat grounds come
out exactly #F04B3A red, #F2EFE7 cream, #302FF5 blue, #E3FF46 lime and #0F0F11 ink, all inside sRGB, while a BT.709
decode pushes red, blue and lime out of gamut (red would clip to #FF5A37). Raw source YUV samples: red 121/96/202
(frame 180), cream 221/124/130 (276), blue 76/215/114 (384), lime 218/46/123 (744), ink 29/129/128 (701).
  Stills are decoded BT.601 limited to full range RGB; measured red #F04B38, cream #F2EFE6, blue #302FF5, lime
  #E3FF44, ink #0F0F11.
  reel.mp4 is converted BT.601 to BT.709 once (swscale accurate_rnd, full_chroma_int) and tagged BT.709, so a
  browser that reads the tags and an HD player that assumes BT.709 both show the source colours; measured BT.709
  decode red #EF4B3A, cream #F2EEE6, blue #2F2FF5, lime #E2FF46, ink #0F0F11 (all within 1 of the source).


TIMING
Frame numbers are decode-order indices 0..899; time is frame / 60. Every source frame already sits on n/60
(0 of 900 off the grid), so the setpts=N/(60*TB) restamp in reel.mp4 moves nothing. Source frame n = reel frame n.
The reel's own timecode (bottom left chrome, HH:MM:SS:FF at 60 fps) equals frame n exactly: "00:00:03:00" at
frame 180, "00:00:10:12" at frame 612. The last frame (899) is a held end card; there is no fade out.

HOW THE FILES WERE MADE (ffmpeg 8, from the local copy S)
  reel.mp4   ffmpeg -colorspace smpte170m -color_range tv -i S -map 0:v:0 -an
               -vf "setpts=N/(60*TB),scale=in_color_matrix=bt601:out_color_matrix=bt709:in_range=tv:out_range=tv:
                    flags=accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int+full_chroma_inp,format=yuv420p"
               -fps_mode cfr -r 60 -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -preset slow -crf 20 -g 30
               -colorspace bt709 -color_primaries bt709 -color_trc bt709 -color_range tv -movflags +faststart
             then a lossless stream copy through h264_metadata (colour_primaries=1, transfer_characteristics=1,
             matrix_coefficients=1, video_full_range_flag=0) so the H.264 VUI carries all three BT.709 tags
  stills     ffmpeg -i S -vf "select='eq(n,N)',scale=W:-2:flags=lanczos+accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int:
               in_color_matrix=bt601:in_range=tv:out_color_matrix=bt601:out_range=pc,format=yuvj444p"
               -frames:v 1 -qmin 1 -q:v 2 out.jpg      (W = 1920 for poster.jpg, 960 for the rest)
