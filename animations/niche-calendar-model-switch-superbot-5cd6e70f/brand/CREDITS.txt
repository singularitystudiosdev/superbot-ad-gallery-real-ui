UI ICONS
  Every Google Calendar UI glyph (menu, search, help, settings, apps, chevrons, add, close, drop-down, check, the
  section arrows, the Focus time headphones, event-repeat, public/globe) is Material Symbols (Google, Apache License
  2.0), fetched from the Iconify API https://api.iconify.design/material-symbols.json on 2026-10-03, path data verbatim
  in scenes/tabs-assets/beats/gm-icons.js. Google Calendar's own icon set.

GOOGLE'S CONSENT STRINGS
  The three scope rows are Google's own descriptions, verbatim from
  https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/scopes (fetched 2026-10-03):
  calendar.events "View and edit events on all your calendars", calendar.settings.readonly "View your Calendar
  settings", gmail.readonly "View your email messages and settings".

GOOGLE CALENDAR LOOK
  Layout and colours of the Calendar screen follow Google's own screenshot "Calendar on web in light mode" in
  "Introducing a new look and feel for Google Calendar on the web", Google Workspace Updates, 2024-10-23,
  https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2024/10/new-look-and-feel-and-dark-mode-google-calendar.html
  (sampled values in scenes/tabs-assets/chat.css). The screenshot itself is not shipped.

  "Nina Alvarez" + "brand consultant" was web-searched on 2026-10-03 (one search each): no notable business or person
  of that name surfaced (exact-name hits were an apartment complex in Marietta, GA called Harbor & Pine and a small
  Etsy vintage shop called Fernwood Goods, neither notable nor in this line of work). No email addresses appear
