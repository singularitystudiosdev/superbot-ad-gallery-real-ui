fly/f_000..095.jpg   The course flyover: 96 frames, 1280x720, rendered headless in Blender 5.2.2 LTS (EEVEE, AgX
hero.jpg             Medium High Contrast) by a bpy build script. hero.jpg is the whole-course still (1920x1080, EEVEE);
solid.jpg            solid.jpg is the same camera in the Workbench engine (the viewport's Solid shading).
                     Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (terrarium encoding, z13, x 2411..2414, y 3080..3085,
                     https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/), whose US source is the USGS 3D
                     Elevation Program (public domain). Scripts in src/ (blender -b --factory-startup -P src/render.9f1df009.py -- frames 96); the Mapzen/AWS Open Data terms ask for source attribution in
                     documentation, which this file is. Box-blurred, 25 m grid, 6x vertical exaggeration
                     (366,261 verts, 365,024 faces). Contour lines every 4 m from the shader.
                     Course: 51 hand-placed waypoints along the 2026 course as CBS New York described it
                     (https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/rbc-brooklyn-half-marathon-2026-route-map-tracker-road-closures-results/,
                     fetched 2026-10-05): Washington Ave, Empire Blvd, Flatbush Ave to Grand Army Plaza and back,
                     Ocean Ave, Parkside Ave, a full counter-clockwise loop of Prospect Park, Ocean Pkwy, Surf Ave,
                     W 10th St, the boardwalk. Measures 13.16 mi; mile posts placed at 1/13.1094 fractions.
                     Approximate, not the certified course.

THE SPOT'S DATA
  The scrape is a dramatisation: the 1,693 pages, the 429s, the 84,117 finishers and the "1:55 negative split"
  finding are made up for the spot (no results site was scraped). The pace plan (1:55:00, park 8:57/mi, Ocean Pkwy
  8:38/mi) is computed from the SPLITS table in scenes/tabs-assets/beats/code.js.
