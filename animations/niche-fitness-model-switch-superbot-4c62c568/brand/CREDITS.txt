SUPERBOT'S OWN TILE ART (first party)
tile-gemini.png, tile-claude.png, tile-openai.webp
             The superbot desktop app's provider tiles, copied unaltered from superbot-desktop
             packages/ui/src/marks/tiles/ (google.png, anthropic.png, openai.webp; the app aliases the Gemini models
             to the google tile and the GPT models to the openai tile). They are what the shipped app paints in the
             switch pill, the "X in superbot" header and the composer's model chip.

GARMIN (third party, unaltered)
