  Scopes (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_messages, instagram_business_content_publish) and the
  authorize endpoint https://www.instagram.com/oauth/authorize: Meta for Developers, "Business Login for Instagram",
  https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/business-login
  (Wayback capture 2026-04-24 14:15:08 UTC). Meta's docs carry no screenshot of the user-facing screen.
  User-facing layout and strings, transcribed from real captures of the screen:
    - ManyChat Help Center, "How to connect Instagram to Manychat",
      https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/14281290924444-How-to-connect-Instagram-to-Manychat
      (screenshot https://help.manychat.com/hc/article_attachments/26386129775132), fetched 2026-10-03:
      "<App> is requesting access to: <account>. If you select Allow, <App> will be able to:",
      "View profile and access media (required)" (locked toggle), "Access and manage messages" (toggle),
      "By allowing, <App> will receive ongoing access to your information and Instagram will record when <App>
      accesses it. Learn More about this sharing and the settings you have.", "<App> Privacy Policy and Terms.",
      buttons "Allow" (blue) and "Cancel" (grey), stacked.
    - Chatwoot Help Center, "How to setup an Instagram channel via Instagram Login",
      https://www.chatwoot.com/hc/user-guide/articles/1744361165-how-to-setup-an-instagram-channel-via-instagram-login
      (Step 3 screenshot), fetched 2026-10-03: the same two permission lines and buttons.
  The spot shows the two lines that are confirmed verbatim. No capture of the content_publish line was found, so it
  is not shown (no guessed wording). The account is shown by display name, never a username.
