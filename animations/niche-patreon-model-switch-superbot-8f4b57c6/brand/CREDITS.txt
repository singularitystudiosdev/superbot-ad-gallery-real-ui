PATREON SOURCES OF RECORD (all fetched 2026-10-03)
  API: https://docs.patreon.com/ (APIv2 scopes: identity "read access to data about the user", campaigns "read access
  to basic campaign data", campaigns.posts "read access to the posts on a campaign"; no endpoint creates or publishes
  a post) and https://www.usecarly.com/blog/claude-patreon-integration/ (Jul 11, 2026: "no way to create a Patreon post
  via the API"). Hence: superbot reads the tiers over OAuth, fills the post in the creator's signed-in editor, and the
  creator clicks Publish.
  OAuth authorize page (https://www.patreon.com/oauth2/authorize): only reachable signed in (a headless load and every
  Wayback capture, e.g. web.archive.org/web/20260913161803/..., show the "Log in or sign up" wall); no screenshot of the
  consent screen was found. Its wording is therefore the scopes' own descriptions above, and the buttons fall back to
  Deny / Allow.
  Post editor: Patreon Help Center "Posting to your Patreon" (support article 115004048046, updated 2026-10-01) with its
  screenshot article_attachments/40564137286413 (close X, Preview post, black Publish pill, Settings sidebar), and
  "Setting post access for your Patreon audience" (37807653033997, updated 2026-10-03) with screenshots
  37929115344013, 37929115344653, 37929099323533 (Audience: Free access / Paid-only access, "Limit access to paid members
  and people who purchase this post", Paid member "Members in selected tiers can access this post", All tiers);
  "Scheduled posts" (360031956632). Palette sampled from those screenshots (chat.css --pt-*).
  On screen the Settings panel keeps only the help center's WORDS as plain text (Audience, "Paid-only access, all 3
  tiers", the three tier names): the real radio buttons, tier checkboxes, "All tiers" dropdown and "Set publish date"
  toggle are left out so no held frame shows a control-shaped element (X ad policy, non-functional UI).
