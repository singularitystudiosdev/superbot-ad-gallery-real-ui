  - Chapters: https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/episode-chapters/ : Episodes > the episode > Details >
    Chapters; manual chapters as "(MM:SS) Title", first at 00:00, at least 3 chapters, at least 30 s apart, titles
    under 40 characters. The seven chapters meet all of it (00:00 first, smallest gap 72 s, longest title 21 chars,
    last 53:10 under 56:32).
  - Distribution: https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/distributing-your-show-to-other-platforms/ and
    /finding-and-enabling-your-rss-feed/ : a show hosted on Spotify is on Spotify automatically; other platforms
    (Apple Podcasts included) need the creator to submit the RSS feed ("we don't distribute automatically"). So the
    Distribution card shows only Spotify (Live) and RSS feed (Updated); no Apple Podcasts row.

    the episode page tabs "Details", "Analytics", "Monetize" (EpisodePage.Nav.*), the "Audio" tag

    and "Processing" (AudioSegmentSubtitle.ProcessingAudioLabel "Processing..."), "Episodes" (EpisodesListScreen.Header).
    The sidebar items Home, Episodes, Analytics and Settings are the sections the help articles name (Go to
