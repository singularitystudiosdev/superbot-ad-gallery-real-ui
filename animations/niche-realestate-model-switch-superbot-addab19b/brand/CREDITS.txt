DATA
  Everything in the spot is made up for it: the user Jordan, the homes, their prices and house numbers, the listing
  details, and every school with its rating: Linden Grove Elementary, Maple Ridge Elementary, Cherry Hill Elementary, Oak
  Creek Middle, Westbrook High, Brookside, Elm Park, Kingsley and Sycamore Bend are invented (checked against the
  Westerville City School District's own school list, https://www.westerville.k12.oh.us/our-district/schools, on
  2026-10-03: "Hawthorne Elementary" is a real Westerville school and was deliberately not used); no rating shown is
  attached to any real school. The ratings are drawn GreatSchools-style (1 to 10 circles, 8 to 10 green, 5 to 7
  amber, 1 to 4 red). The 214 homes, 11 near good schools and 8 that pass the photo check are made-up counts. The street names are real Westerville, OH
  streets, taken from the US Census Bureau's TIGER/Line data (public domain, US federal government work). Any
  resemblance of a made-up home, price or person to a real one is coincidental. The house photos are real photographs
  credited in ../img/CREDITS.txt and stand in for the invented listings; their real addresses are not known or used.
