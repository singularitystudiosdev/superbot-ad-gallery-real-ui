STRIPE: NO LOGO. The Stripe tile, the "Connecting to Stripe" pill, the composer chip and the superbot label bar use the
  plain word "Stripe" in the superbot UI font. Stripe's Mark Usage Terms (https://stripe.com/legal/marks, last updated
  June 12, 2023) allow the logo "only on the portion of your website or application that directly relates to our
  services (such as on a checkout page)" and the word mark "without alteration in text to truthfully and accurately
  refer to us"; an ad is not such a portion, so only the word is used. No "official", "partner" or endorsement wording.

UI ICONS
  The neutral UI glyphs (file, git-branch, check, check-circle-fill, repo-push, inbox, gear, search, sync, credit-card,
  mail, link-external) are Octicons (GitHub, MIT License, https://github.com/primer/octicons), fetched from the Iconify
  API https://api.iconify.design/octicon.json on 2026-10-03, path data verbatim (scenes/tabs-assets/beats/glyphs.js).
  The badge ticks/crosses, the two-way arrow and the chevrons on the install card are plain SVG strokes drawn in
  stripe.js (UI furniture). The account icon is a letter tile ("P"), as Stripe shows an account without a logo.

    https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/api-authentication/oauth (oauth-user-journey.0fd6041638a1cbb305dc88690354a462.png)
    https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/install-links (oauth-permissions.9f11ce1ba29fdd77c9d4fa9ee2944222.png,

    https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/reference/permissions; the confirm label "Install" from
    https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-apps/upload-install-app ("then click Install").
  Checklist: "Smart Retries" (https://docs.stripe.com/billing/revenue-recovery/smart-retries); failed payment emails
    ("Send emails when card payments fail", https://docs.stripe.com/billing/revenue-recovery/customer-emails).
  Dashboard: light theme; type is the Dashboard's own system stack read from its public stylesheets
    (b.stripecdn.com/dashboard-fe-statics-srv/assets/*.css via https://dashboard.stripe.com/login): -apple-system,
    BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Ubuntu, sans-serif; Menlo for ids. No font files
    bundled. Status labels are Stripe's own: subscriptions Active / Past due, payments Succeeded / Failed
    (https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/overview).
