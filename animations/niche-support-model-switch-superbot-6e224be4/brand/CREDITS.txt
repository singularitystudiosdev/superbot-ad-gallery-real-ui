ZENDESK'S OAUTH AUTHORIZATION PAGE (sources of record)
  The card follows the page an app sends the user to, https://{subdomain}.zendesk.com/oauth/authorizations/new
  (Zendesk help "Using OAuth authentication with your application",
  https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408845965210, fetched 2026-10-03: "The page asks the user to authorize
  your application to access Zendesk on their behalf"; the client's Name and Company are what "users will see when
  asked to grant access"; the card shows the app's name, with no owner line). The two rows name the scopes superbot asks for (scope=read write, the article's own example),
  in the article's wording: "The read scope gives an app access to GET endpoints" (row "Read", "Access to GET
  endpoints"); "The write scope gives an app access to POST, PUT, and DELETE endpoints for creating, updating, and
  deleting resources" (row "Write", "Creating, updating, and deleting resources"). The live page sits behind a Zendesk
  login and the docs carry no screenshot of it, so its heading and its Allow / Deny button labels could not be checked
  against the live page; developer.zendesk.com's OAuth pages answered 404 / client-rendered stubs to a headless fetch.
  No string contains a dash, so no line was split.
