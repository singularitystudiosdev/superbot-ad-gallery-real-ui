YOUTUBE STUDIO UI AND PALETTE (sources of record)
  Analytics page grammar: YouTube Studio's per-video "Audience retention" report (the retention curve against a grey
  "Typical retention" band, "Key moments" for intro, dips and spikes), per YouTube Help "Measure key moments for
  audience retention" https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415 . Tabs, the date drop-down, "See more" and
  "Advanced mode" are left out (policy guard: nothing on screen reads as a control).
  Palette: YouTube's own --yt-sys-color-baseline--* light values, read from the stylesheets www.youtube.com serves
  (headless Chromium, 2026-10-03): base-background #fff, text-primary #0f0f0f, text-secondary #606060, outline
  rgba(0,0,0,.1), outline-opaque #e5e5e5, additive-background rgba(0,0,0,.05), call-to-action #065fd4,
  inverted-background #0f0f0f. Set in chat.css --yt-*. The chart itself is a data plot drawn as SVG from data.js.
