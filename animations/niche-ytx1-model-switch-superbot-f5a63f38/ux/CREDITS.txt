ux/ CREDITS (internal provenance; nothing here is shown on screen)

What ux/ draws: YouTube Studio's channel "Community" page, Comments tab, LIGHT theme, inside a framed browser window
on superbot's dark stage (ux/studio.js, ux/studio.css, ux/icons.js, ux/preview.html). Every pixel is HTML/CSS/SVG
drawn here; no screenshot below is embedded in the ad. They were references for layout, type, spacing, icons and
wording only. All fetched 2026-10-03. Local copies live in /tmp/ytx1-3ff9c352/ux/refs/ (scratch, not shipped).

Why light theme: the newest channel-level Community page frames (Nov 2025) and the newest Video comments frames
(Sep 2026) are both light; light is also Studio's default and what YouTube Help illustrates. The dark frames (Aug 2026)
were used only to confirm the "Pinned by" label and row anatomy, which are identical across themes.

Video frames were downloaded with yt-dlp (video track only, <=1080p) and single frames cut with
ffmpeg -ss <seconds> -frames:v 1. "t=" is the frame's time in the source video. Watch URLs open at that time.
