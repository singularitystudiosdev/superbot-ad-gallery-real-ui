This folder rebuilds the real YouTube Studio and YouTube watch page surfaces as layered HTML/CSS (ux.js, ux.css,
icons.js) for the fictional creator channel "Sam Rivera". Nothing in it is a screenshot: the references below were
read for layout, wording and measurements only, and none of them is shipped in this folder. The reference files are
kept in the build scratch, /tmp/ytx3-ux.b343d06b/refs/. Every reference was retrieved on 2026-10-03.
