  "From the left menu, select Content. Click the title or thumbnail of the video you'd like to edit. From the left
  menu, select Editor"; "Select Trim & cut New Cut. A red box will appear in the editor. Drag the sides of the red box
  ... To confirm your edits, select [the check]. Click Save"; "You can click Discard changes at any time"; the video's
  URL, view count and comments stay the same; the over-100,000-views save restriction "does not apply to channels who
  are in the YouTube Partner Program" (Maya Makes is: Earn is in her menu).

  the Description, add a list of timestamps and titles", the first at 0:00, at least three, each at least 10 seconds,
  "Click SAVE". Hence 0:00 Intro stays and the nine after it move back 25 s.

  views and the "% of viewers are still watching at 0:30" stat.
