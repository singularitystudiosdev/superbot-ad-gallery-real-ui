TIMING
Frame numbers below are decode-order indices 0..1909 and times are frame / 60, which is exactly where each frame sits
in showreel-full.mp4. The source is 60 fps except at the reel splice, where two timestamps are duplicated: source
frames 952 and 953 both carry pts 15.8667 s and frames 955 and 956 both carry 15.9333 s (nothing at 15.8833 or
15.9167). showreel-full.mp4 restamps every frame to n/60 (setpts=N/(60*TB)), so frames 953 and 955 each move by one
60th of a second; no frame is dropped or repeated and the length stays 31.833 s. Source frame n = clip frame n.

decode untagged 1080p, so the 709 column is the colour a page shows for the video. The 601 column is a BT.601 decode
(ffmpeg's default for untagged input); the chat-rule hexes match that decode, not the browser.
