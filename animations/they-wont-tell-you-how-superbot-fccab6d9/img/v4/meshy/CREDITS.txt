img/v4/meshy/ : Meshy beat imagery for they-wont-tell-you-how-superbot-fccab6d9

Every image in this folder is a real 3D mesh render, made offline with headless three.js (clay, wireframe and
textured passes; 448x320 webp turntables at 36 frames per revolution, 224x224 clay candidates, 320x240 textured
strip thumbnails). Nothing is hand drawn and nothing is a screenshot.

Geometry: Meshy community models from the public showcase feed of meshy.ai (read through the site's own unsigned
showcase endpoint, geometry from each entry's quad JSON, baked to position + quad index buffers, n-gons fan
triangulated). No Meshy account, API key or credits were used. All four are published by their authors as CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); CC0 needs no attribution, authors are listed for the record.
Triangle counts are the counts Meshy publishes for each asset.

  https://www.meshy.ai/discover/0197ad11-12b3-796a-803a-383b791f6f73
  "A stylized 3D model of a teenage boy in a chibi form, wearing a green hoodie" by ktmarine1999. Licence: CC0 1.0.
  Chosen as the closest CC0 match to the round-headed Pocketsflow halftone mascot.

Materials: the feed ships no UVs, so no texture atlas is projected. The textured pass is an authored PBR material per
region, split on the geometry (mascot skin by a surface flood fill from the face, sculpted eye shapes, hair, hoodie,
trousers, sneakers by height; phone screen, frame and back by face normal; cart wheels by height), plus a cavity bake
computed from the mesh. Lighting: one soft key, a cool rim, a low fill, three.js RoomEnvironment, dark grid floor.
Renderer: three.js 0.180 (MIT), harness derived from .tmp/meshy-80e24d9f/render/meshy.html + shoot3.mjs.
