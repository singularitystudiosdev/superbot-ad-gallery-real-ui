This spot ships no photographic or third-party image assets. Everything on screen — the hub chrome, the tray
of app tiles, the winnings site's chrome, its chart, its payout rows and the confetti burst — is drawn in
CSS/SVG/canvas from code in this folder.

The winnings shown (the $48,210 total, the "biggest hits" payouts, the biggest-night figure) and the accounts
they would have come from are fictional. No sportsbook or casino brand name or logo appears anywhere in the
spot: the venues in the wins list are described generically ("high limit room", "final table"). The
"gamble" line is a quote about advice, not an offer, and no game, odds, payout or bonus is shown or promised.
